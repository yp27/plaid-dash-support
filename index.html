A clear view.
A little atmosphere.

Plaid Dash turns your iPhone or iPad into a GPS speed display. Choose the flowing starfield of PLAID or the quiet amber dial of VECTOR.

Free to use. No account, ads, or in-app purchases.

GET STARTED

Your display, ready in a few taps.

  1. Preview a theme, then select Use This Theme.
  2. Choose km/h or mph in Settings. Turn on Mirror Display if you want to try a windshield reflection.
  3. Mount your device securely, open Drive Mode, and allow location access while using the app.

Configure the display before setting off. GPS speed is a supplemental reference and does not replace your vehicle’s instruments.

Why does the speed show “—”?

Plaid Dash has no fresh, reliable GPS reading. Check location permission and try a location with a clear view of the sky. Tunnels, buildings, and indoor use can reduce signal quality. The app clears stale readings instead of displaying a made-up speed.

Where did the controls go?

Controls fade after 3 seconds in a theme preview and 5 seconds in Drive Mode. Tap the screen to bring them back. Controls remain visible while adjusting a slider, or while VoiceOver is enabled.

How do I adjust brightness?

In Drive Mode, use the brightness slider or swipe vertically. Night appearance limits brightness. Leaving Drive Mode or moving the app to the background restores the brightness and Auto-Lock setting captured at the start of that session.

Can I use it without a Tesla?

Yes. Speed comes from your device’s GPS. No vehicle connection or Tesla account is required. This version does not connect to Tesla Fleet API and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla.

How can I reduce animation?

Turn off motion in Settings or choose VECTOR. PLAID also pauses decorative motion when stationary, without a valid speed, in Low Power Mode, or under serious thermal pressure. Continuous GPS and a bright screen still use battery.

What devices are supported?

This release requires iOS or iPadOS 26.2 or later. Drive Mode uses landscape orientation. GPS availability and accuracy depend on your device and signal conditions; some iPad models do not have built-in GPS.

PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 8, 2026

Your drive stays on your device.

This policy describes the Plaid Dash iPhone and iPad app, developed by Peng Yang.

Location

The app uses foreground location while Drive Mode is active to calculate GPS speed. It does not save location history or send location to the developer or a server. Theme previews use clearly identified simulated speed and do not start GPS.

Preferences

Theme, speed units, language, appearance, brightness, mirror, and motion preferences are stored locally. You can change them in Settings. There is no app account to create or delete.

No advertising or tracking

The app includes no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or app-operated data upload. It does not sell personal data. Apple may process diagnostics or TestFlight feedback according to your Apple settings and Apple’s own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can change location permission in your device’s Settings. Without permission, you can still preview themes, but live GPS speed is unavailable. Leaving Drive Mode or backgrounding the app stops its location updates.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Do not include location history or other sensitive information. This website’s hosting provider may process standard request information needed to serve and protect the site; the app itself does not contact this website automatically.

Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
