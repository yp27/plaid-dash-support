PRIVACY POLICY · NOVEMBER 1, 2026

Clear choices for your driving data.

This policy describes Plaid Dash for iPhone and iPad, developed by Peng Yang. GPS, Bluetooth and previews can be used without a cloud account. Optional Tesla cloud connectivity has different data handling, described below.

Local GPS, Bluetooth and previews

Foreground location supports GPS speed, elevation and cycling information. The app does not upload phone GPS coordinates, routes or location history to our cloud service. Bluetooth readings are used locally; Bluetooth vehicle keys are stored in the device Keychain. Preferences are stored locally. Previews use simulated readings and do not require a vehicle or location permission. Backgrounding stops location updates.

Optional Tesla cloud connection

If you choose to connect through Tesla OAuth, our service processes authorization tokens, account/grant and app-device session identifiers, authorized vehicle identifiers including VIN, permission scopes and selected vehicle information to authenticate you and provide the requested features. The app-scoped device identifier is used for session management, not advertising.

Depending on your permissions and vehicle support, vehicle information may include battery and range summaries, temperatures, tire and door/window status, lights, current media information, and navigation destination text with remaining distance and time. Navigation destination text can reveal places you plan to visit. We do not store GPS coordinates, route polylines or a trip history. Cloud access is optional and readings depend on vehicle, software, permissions and freshness.

Retention and removal

Cloud authorization, vehicle associations and sessions are kept in access-controlled encrypted storage. App sessions have a 30-day validity and may renew when used. Battery/range summaries expire within 30 days; additional summary caches expire within 30 minutes and are replaced by newer data. Live services retain selected latest states and synchronization markers rather than raw telemetry history. Expired records are no longer served; physical cleanup is request-driven and managed database backups follow the hosting provider's configured retention.

Use the app's cloud disconnect/sign-out controls to remove the associated session and caches. You can also revoke authorization through your Tesla account and contact support for a data-removal request. Removing a local session does not by itself prove that an offline vehicle has removed its telemetry configuration; any pending cleanup requires the vehicle to come online.

No advertising or tracking

We do not sell personal data, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or combine your data with third-party data for advertising tracking. Optional cloud data is used to provide and protect the requested app functionality. Apple may process diagnostics or TestFlight feedback under your Apple settings and its own privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Apple-verified transactions on your device unlock the theme pack and restore access. We do not receive payment-card details or upload purchase events to a developer-operated analytics service. Separately offered paid cloud services are not included in the theme pack.

Your choices

You can change location and Bluetooth permissions in system Settings, use previews without permissions, and use GPS/Bluetooth without connecting a cloud account. Configure the app while parked. Revoking a cloud authorization does not delete Apple's purchase records.

Support and this website

When you choose to email support, we use the email address and information you provide to respond. Feedback drafts are not automatically sent. Do not include passwords, tokens or sensitive location history. Hosting providers may process standard request information to serve and protect this website.

Changes

We update this page when our practices change. The date above identifies this revision.
